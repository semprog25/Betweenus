# Product Context

## Why it exists
People need a place to share secrets, gossip, confessions, and messy stories without tying them to a public real-world identity — while remaining accountable to the platform.

## Feel
Simple, premium, mysterious, social, entertaining, emotionally intelligent, private, safe, fast.

## Differentiators
- Me Too (shared experience, not generic Like)
- People Who Get It
- The Tea
- Between Us Journal (editorial)
- Anonymous human connection

## UX references (principles only)
- SayNothing.io → simplicity
- VentSpace → emotional polish (not therapy)
- Ispovesti → story discovery

## Roles
- **Website:** discovery + SEO + Journal + acquisition (usable without the app)
- **App:** community + interaction + retention
- **Supabase:** shared auth + data + authorization + security
