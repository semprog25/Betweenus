What you can do

Anonymous Stories — browse real community posts without public identity branding.

Spill — write what you have been carrying and publish to the feed after sign-in.

React & Discuss — vote and reply once you join.

Daily Check-ins — private mood tracking that never belongs on the public feed.

Between Us Journal — editorial essays separate from community Stories.

Guides — practical resources on anonymous sharing, tough conversations, and healthier discussion norms.

Why Between Us exists

Named social feeds reward performance. Between Us separates your account (used to sign in) from what you publish as Anonymous, so honesty does not require building a brand around your most vulnerable moments. Learn more on the Journey page.
